# Self Made — Video Episode 01 (Lean-Vox Script)

**Working title:** "I analyzed [N] winning Facebook ads. They all do the same 3 things."
**Runtime:** ~4–6 min · **Format:** lean-Vox (screen-recording + real ads + data, AI or founder VO)
**Goal:** rank on YouTube for ad-strategy searches + demo the corpus + drive free signups.

> **Before you shoot — fill the 4 real numbers** (from your DB, so it's true & specific):
> `[N]` = ads analyzed · `[X%]` = share showing the pattern · `[$Y]` = a real daily spend on a shown ad · `[BRAND]` = a real brand from your corpus. Everything else is ready to read.

---

## THUMBNAIL / TITLE (pick 1 — A/B test)
- **A:** "I analyzed [N] winning ads" + your face reacting + a wall of ad thumbnails behind
- **B:** "Why every [niche] ad looks the SAME" + 2 near-identical ads side by side with a red circle
- **C:** "$[Y]/day — decoded" over a single scroll-stopping ad frame

Rule: thumbnail promises a *secret pattern*. The video pays it off.

---

## THE SCRIPT (VO = voiceover · ON-SCREEN = what the viewer sees)

### 🎬 COLD OPEN — 0:00–0:12 (the hook; do NOT intro yourself yet)
**VO:**
> "This ad is spending [$Y] a day. This one's barely spending anything. Same product. Same audience. The difference isn't the budget — it's three things, and once you see them, you can't unsee them."

**ON-SCREEN:** Fast cut between 2 real ads from your Discovery grid — one with a high spend/active badge, one without. Freeze. A big number `[$Y]/day` slams onto the winner. Quick zoom.
**Editing note:** hard cut on the beat of "three things." No logo yet — earn the first 10 seconds.

---

### SETUP — 0:12–0:45 (establish authority + the corpus flex)
**VO:**
> "I run a database of over [N] Facebook and TikTok ads — every one pulled straight from the ad libraries, tagged by what it's spending and how long it's been running. So instead of guessing what works, I sorted for the ads that are actually still live and scaling… and looked for what they had in common. [X%] of them did the exact same three things. Here they are."

**ON-SCREEN:** Screen-record scrolling your Discovery grid *fast* (hundreds of ads flying past) → a counter ticking up to `[N]` → filter clicks to "longest running / highest spend" → grid re-sorts. This IS the product demo, disguised as research.
**Editing note:** the scrolling wall of real ads is your Vox "data visualization." It's free and no competitor can fake it.

---

### PATTERN 1 — 0:45–1:45 · "The first frame does one job"
**VO:**
> "Number one: the winners don't start with a logo, a slow pan, or a pretty shot. The first frame creates a question or an emotion in under a second. A face mid-reaction. A problem shown, not told. Look —"

**ON-SCREEN:** Show 4–5 real winning first-frames from your corpus in a 2x2 grid, each frozen at 0:00. Circle the face / the problem / the text hook in red. Then show 2 weak ones (slow logo intro) with a grey ✕.
**Editing note:** side-by-side "winner vs loser" is the Vox move. Annotate directly on the ad.

---

### PATTERN 2 — 1:45–2:50 · "They earn attention before they sell"
**VO:**
> "Number two: the sale comes late. The average winner spends the first several seconds giving you a reason to keep watching — a demonstration, a transformation, a 'wait, what?' moment — before it ever mentions the brand. The losers pitch in the first two seconds. And the algorithm punishes them for it, because nobody sticks around."

**ON-SCREEN:** Play 2–3 short clips (5s each) of winning ads hitting a "satisfying reveal" (product demo / before-after). Overlay a simple timeline bar showing "hook → payoff → *then* brand." Compare to a loser that shows the logo at 0:01.
**Editing note:** the timeline bar overlay = cheap motion graphic, big Vox energy.

---

### PATTERN 3 — 2:50–3:50 · "They look native, not produced"
**VO:**
> "Number three — and this is the one most brands get wrong: the top ads don't look like ads. They look like something a real person filmed. Vertical, a little rough, UGC-style. I pulled the highest-spending [BRAND] ads and almost none of them are the polished studio shots. They're the ones that blend into the feed."

**ON-SCREEN:** Pull up `[BRAND]` in Brand Spy → show their live ad set → highlight that the scaled ones are UGC-style. Grid of "looks like an ad ✕" vs "looks native ✓".
**Editing note:** naming a real brand + showing real live ads = credibility no stock example can match.

---

### THE TWIST / DEEPER INSIGHT — 3:50–4:40 (this is what makes it *share-worthy*)
**VO:**
> "But here's the part nobody tells you. Knowing the pattern isn't the hard part — *doing it fast enough* is. The brands that win aren't more creative. They just ship more variations of the winning pattern, faster, and cut the losers quicker. Speed is the strategy. So the real question is: how fast can you turn a winning ad you found… into ten of your own?"

**ON-SCREEN:** Quick montage — find a winner in Discovery → hit "clone" → AI generates a variation → side-by-side of original vs your new version. Keep it fast and real.
**Editing note:** THIS is the soft product reveal. Don't say "buy my tool." Show the workflow answering the question you just posed.

---

### CTA — 4:40–5:10 (earned, not begged)
**VO:**
> "I built the tool I use to do all of this — search the ads, see what's spending, and clone the winners in a couple clicks. There's a free plan, link's in the description. Steal the pattern, ship faster than your competitors, and I'll see you in the next one."

**ON-SCREEN:** Clean end card: product name, "Free plan → [link]", and 2 video thumbnails to click next. Your logo *finally* front and center.
**Editing note:** first and only hard CTA. One ask. Free plan lowers the barrier.

---

## 📱 SHORTS EXTRACTION (cut these from the same footage — your daily beat)
1. **"This ad spends [$Y]/day. Here's why."** — Cold open + Pattern 1 only (0:00–1:45 trimmed to 40s)
2. **"Stop selling in the first 2 seconds"** — Pattern 2 as a standalone 30s
3. **"Your ads look too good"** — Pattern 3, the native-vs-polished flip (30s)
4. **"Speed is the strategy"** — the twist, ending on the clone montage (45s)
→ 1 long video = 4 Shorts = ~2 weeks of daily posts across Reels/TikTok/Shorts.

## PRODUCTION CHECKLIST (lean)
- [ ] Pull the 4 real numbers from the DB (`[N] [X%] [$Y] [BRAND]`)
- [ ] Screen-record: Discovery scroll, filter sort, Brand Spy, clone workflow (this is 80% of your footage)
- [ ] VO: record yourself OR ElevenLabs (pick one voice, keep it consistent across episodes — it becomes the brand)
- [ ] Edit: CapCut/Descore/Premier — annotations (red circles, grey ✕, the timeline bar) are the only "motion graphics" you need
- [ ] Music: subtle, driving, no lyrics
- [ ] Total effort target: **1 day** shoot+edit for the long-form, 1 hour for the 4 Shorts

---

## WHY THIS EPISODE FIRST
It's the strongest possible opener: it's *useful* (real patterns), it's *provable* (real data from your corpus), it *demos the product without pitching*, and the "speed is the strategy" twist sets up the clone tool as the obvious answer. Every future episode reuses this exact skeleton — just swap the topic (niches, specific brands, seasonal, "worst ads" teardowns). The format is the asset.
