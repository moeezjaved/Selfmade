# Self Made — Go-To-Market Strategy, Marketing & Sales Plan, Revenue Model

Bootstrapped, solo-founder GTM for an ad-intelligence + AI-ad-creation SaaS. Grounded in verified 2026
market research (see caveats) + everything built in this project. Pricing: Free / $39 / $99 / $249 / Enterprise.

## 0. The honest market read (verified research)

- **Category is validated and bootstrappable.** Motion reached 1,000+ customers analyzing $6B+ in ad spend;
  **Foreplay reached 30,000+ users and 7-figure ARR on $0 external funding.** A solo/bootstrapped path to
  seven figures is proven in this exact niche.
- **ICP is large.** ~5.5M Shopify merchants; ~$240B US DTC e-commerce. Buyers = performance marketers, DTC/
  Shopify brands, media buyers, agencies. (Do NOT cite a "category $ size / CAGR" — every such figure was
  refuted as market-mill junk; anchor TAM in these ICP counts.)
- **The proven GTM here is content + community + founder-led, NOT paid acquisition.** Motion grew on the
  *Thumbstop* newsletter (50k weekly ad-pro readers) + Creative Strategy Summit (17,500 registrants); Foreplay
  on a sharp product hook (permanent cross-platform ad saving) + PLG + a Chrome extension; Minea on a
  30%-lifetime-commission affiliate program. **None of these required an ad budget — that's the opening.**

## 1. GTM strategy

### ICP & beachhead (win this segment FIRST)
Full market = performance marketers, DTC/Shopify brands, media buyers, agencies. Don't boil the ocean —
**beachhead = solo & small-team DTC performance marketers + small agencies who spy on ads *daily* and need to
create fast.** This is the segment Atria prices out ($129–959) and that Foreplay/Motion only half-serve
(save-only / analytics-only). They're high-intent, price-sensitive, and live in the Meta Ad Library.

### Positioning wedge
> **"Spy → clone → launch → track ROAS — in one tool, at a fifth of Atria's price."**

| Competitor | What they do | The gap Self Made fills |
|---|---|---|
| Atria ($129–959) | Intelligence only, premium price | You: same breadth, 1/5 price |
| Foreplay | Save / spy only | You: also create + launch |
| Motion ($250+) | Analytics only | You: also spy + create |
| GetHookd ($29–129) | Spy + create | You: also launch + ROAS |

**Your unfair advantages:** (1) a 5M-ad corpus (Atria-scale breadth), (2) AI clone at Nano Banana Pro quality
(verified $0.15/image, usable ads), (3) end-to-end spy→clone→launch→track that no single competitor offers.
**You are the only tool that closes the loop.**

## 2. Marketing plan — ranked by ROI for a bootstrapped solo founder

**1. Owned-audience content engine — the #1 channel (Motion's proven model).**
A weekly **"winning ads teardown"** across newsletter + Twitter/X + short-form video: "here's an ad spending
$X/day and *why* it works." It's the format that converts *this* audience, it's near-zero cost, and it builds
an owned list you control. This is your Thumbstop. Start it *pre-launch*.

**2. Programmatic SEO off your 5M-ad corpus — your unfair, near-zero-CAC engine.**
Publish public `/brands/[slug]` pages and a free ad-library search — potentially *hundreds of thousands* of
indexable pages built from data you already have. Competitors can't cheaply match a 5M-ad corpus. This is a
compounding organic-acquisition moat unique to you. (Ties to the programmatic-SEO plan already in the roadmap;
keep ≥5–10 ads/page to avoid thin-content penalties.)

**3. Free tool + Chrome extension (Foreplay's hook).**
A free ad-search + "save this ad forever" Chrome extension as top-of-funnel. Foreplay's entire wedge was
"save ads from anywhere, forever (no expired links)." A free tool that solves one sharp pain → email → nurture
to paid.

**4. Build-in-public — you're already doing it.**
Document the 5M-ad crawl, the AI clone results, the founder journey on Twitter/X + LinkedIn. Founders in this
space (Foreplay's Zach Murray) built audiences this way. Cost: $0, just consistency.

**5. Affiliate program — 30% lifetime commission (Minea's model).**
Near-zero-CAC. Launch *after* your first paying cohort proves retention. Add a "free plan at N referrals"
viral loop.

**6. Product Hunt launch** — one-time spike + credibility + backlinks. Time it to your public launch.

**7. Community** — be genuinely useful in media-buyer Discords/Slacks, DTC Twitter, r/PPC, FB ad groups.
No spam; teardowns + answers → profile → signups.

**Deprioritize:** paid ads (you can't afford them, and it's not how this niche wins). Revisit only once you
have proven LTV to spend against.

**Content formats that convert this ICP:** ad teardowns · "why this ad works" breakdowns · winner case
studies · free swipe files/tools · YouTube/short-form teardowns.

## 3. Sales plan

- **Primary = self-serve PLG.** Free → 7-day trial → paid. The product sells itself: Free tier + welcome
  credits create the "I cloned a winning ad in 60 seconds" activation moment; the credit cap + gated
  intelligence/launch features drive the upgrade. **No sales team year 1.**
- **Secondary = light founder-led sales for Business/Enterprise.** Agencies want more seats + brands; do warm
  outreach + live demos yourself for $249+/custom deals. This is where the biggest logos come from.
- **Activation is the whole game** — instrument "time to first cloned ad" and "first Brand-Spy add." Users who
  hit those convert far better. Onboard straight into a clone.

### The single biggest lever: churn
No verified niche churn benchmark exists, and it dominates the 24-month outcome. At 8% monthly churn you leak
customers as fast as you add them; at 4% you compound. **Retention features (brand alerts, weekly digest,
winner alerts — already on your roadmap) are worth more than any acquisition channel.** Model, then measure it
from day one.

## 5. The 90-day launch sequence

**Days 0–30 (pre-launch):** ship the free ad-search tool + Chrome extension + first programmatic-SEO pages;
start the weekly teardown newsletter/Twitter; build a waitlist. Reach 5M ads so the corpus is credible.

**Days 31–60 (launch):** Product Hunt + newsletter blast + open the free plan. Onboard everyone into a first
cloned ad (activation).

**Days 61–90 (compound):** publish 2–3 teardowns/week, launch the 30%-lifetime affiliate program, close 3–5
agency deals via founder outreach, and instrument activation + churn.

## 6. Risks & honest caveats
- **Churn is unquantified for this niche** and is the model's biggest swing — prioritize retention.
- **Competition is real and funded** (Atria, Foreplay, Motion). Win on price + end-to-end + the SEO/corpus moat, not features alone.
- **Solo-founder bandwidth** — the content engine and the product both need you; the programmatic-SEO + newsletter are chosen precisely because they scale without headcount.
- **Research caveats:** competitor scale figures are self-reported and time-sensitive; conversion benchmarks are cross-SaaS, not niche-specific; the Bannerbear pacing is a single adjacent comparable. Treat the numbers as a calibrated model, not a promise — and update the assumptions with *your* real funnel data within 90 days.

## 7. How the bootstrappers actually operated (the cadence behind the strategy)

The strategy above is *what*. This is *the rhythm*. None of these companies had a big team early —
they won by doing a small number of things **every single day/week without missing.**

- **Foreplay (Zach Murray):** shipped a sharp free-ish hook, then showed up daily on Twitter/X + in
  media-buyer communities answering questions and posting ad breakdowns. The engine was *consistency of
  presence*, not volume of channels. One product wedge + daily community presence → 30k users.
- **Motion:** treated content like a product. The *Thumbstop* newsletter went out on a **fixed weekly
  cadence** — reliability is what compounded a list to 50k. Plus one flagship event/year (the Summit).
- **Minea:** made the affiliate program the growth loop — recruit affiliates weekly, they market for you.

**The pattern = a fixed weekly content beat + daily audience presence + a compounding loop (SEO/affiliate).**
For a solo founder the whole job is protecting that rhythm from the product work.

### Daily plan (~60–90 min total, before you touch code)
| Slot | Time | What |
|---|---|---|
| **Morning: presence (30 min)** | 1st thing | Post **1 ad teardown / insight** on X + LinkedIn (pull a winner from your own corpus — you have 5M ads, this is free content forever). Reply to 5–10 media-buyer posts with genuine value. |
| **Midday: community (20 min)** | lunch | Answer 2–3 questions in a media-buyer Discord/Slack/r/PPC/FB group. No pitching — just be the most useful person in the room. Drop your teardown link only where it fits. |
| **Evening: pipeline (15 min)** | EOD | DM/email **2 warm agency or brand leads** (demo offer). Reply to every trial signup personally. Log 1 activation/churn metric. |
| **Always-on** | — | Every teardown ends with a soft CTA to the free tool/waitlist. |

The daily block is deliberately small so it survives busy build days. **Missing a day is the only real failure mode.**

### Weekly plan
| Day | Focus | Deliverable |
|---|---|---|
| **Mon** | Content production | Write the week's **teardown newsletter** (your Thumbstop). Batch 3–5 short posts from it. |
| **Tue** | SEO / moat | Ship/expand **programmatic-SEO pages** off the corpus. Check indexation + top landing pages in Search Console. |
| **Wed** | Product-led growth | Improve one **activation** step (time-to-first-clone). Ship one free-tool/extension improvement. |
| **Thu** | Sales | **Send the newsletter.** Founder-led outreach: 5–10 agency/brand demos booked or run. |
| **Fri** | Loops + review | Recruit 2–3 **affiliates**. Review the week's numbers (signups, free→paid, churn, top content). Pick next week's teardown topics. |
| **Weekend** | Light presence only | Keep posting (scheduled), rest the rest. |

### Weekly scorecard (track these 6 — nothing else early)
1. Free signups  2. Free→paid conversion %  3. New MRR  4. **Monthly churn %** (the one that decides everything)
5. Activation rate (% who clone an ad in week 1)  6. Newsletter subs + open rate.

### Monthly beats
- **Month 1:** launch the newsletter + free tool, build waitlist (pre-launch).
- **Month 2:** Product Hunt launch + open free plan.
- **Month 3:** launch the 30%-lifetime affiliate program; publish first customer case study.
- **Every month after:** one "big rock" (a flagship teardown series, a mini-tool, an integration) + keep the weekly rhythm.

**The honest math:** this cadence is ~10 focused hours/week on GTM. That's the price of the base-case
trajectory — the revenue model assumes you protect it. Skip the weekly beat and you fall to the conservative line.
