Selfmade · the S, as a spaceship

The S is the flight path.

A rocket leaves a trail. Make that trail an S and you get both at once: a ship in motion, and the initial of the name. You read the rocket first, the letter a beat later — which is exactly what makes a mark stick. Bold cream-on-orange, tested at 16px, night, and sticker.

My pick: 01 · Contrail-S. It’s the cleanest read of both ideas — a bold S with a sharp nose at the leading tip and two exhaust puffs at the tail, so it’s obviously a ship that just flew an S. It stays legible at 16px and looks fast. 02 · Rocket + plume is the most obviously a spaceship (upright rocket) with the S in the smoke, best for stickers/merch. 03 · S-Craft turns the S itself into the ship (nose + flame).
