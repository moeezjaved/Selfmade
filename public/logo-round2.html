Identity study · round two · the discovered S

An S you find, not one we drew.

Twelve new constructions. In every one, the S appears as a consequence of something moving by itself — a split, a rotation, a trail, a seam — never as a typed letter. Judged for 16px legibility, app-icon presence, sticker desirability, and single-colour embroidery.

Round two — twelve constructions

Each caption names the mechanism that produces the S. If you can't find the S in two seconds at tile size, the concept fails.

The strongest five — and the hidden idea in each

Top three, applied — icon · night · header · favicon · sticker · poster · merch

My verdict: 01 · Seam. It keeps everything you liked in Drift — two solid shapes, sliding motion, unbreakable at 16px — but the two halves now interlock through an S-shaped joint, so the S is genuinely discovered: you see a split disc first, the letter a half-second later. That delay is what makes marks iconic. It also carries the deepest story in the set: the founder half and the machine half, cut from one circle, each holding the other. Runner-up: 02 · Reform — the cleanest "loop rearranged itself into an S" and the best sticker. 03 · Halfmade is the name made visible (half solid, half still forming) and worth keeping as a secondary/animated mark.
