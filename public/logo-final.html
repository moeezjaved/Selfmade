Selfmade identity · final direction · the Channel S

The mark, refined to production.

Five tight refinements of the one direction — a disc split by an S-shaped channel, so the letter is discovered, never pasted in. They differ only in gap, weight, corner-softness and tilt, so they read as one family. Each is shown across the full system: symbol, lockup, 16px favicon, app icon, sticker, orange-on-cream, cream-on-orange, and night.
